# threshold and leave wrong-layout text standing.

# Every line is normalised by the same rules as the corpus — tatweel and
# harakat stripped, أإآٱ folded to ا, ى to ي, ة to ه — so the natural spelling
# is what belongs here.
#
# One word per line. Add only words you are sure of: an entry here is shipped
# to everyone and vouches for a reading, so a misspelling teaches the detector
# to accept a mistake.
